The salon kit, whole: hourly scale, fifteen-minute slots, ten minutes between appointments, an hour's lead, sixty days ahead, confirmed automatically, cancellable until three hours before, no online payment. Noa keeps her own hours (Tuesday to Saturday); the others follow the salon's.
